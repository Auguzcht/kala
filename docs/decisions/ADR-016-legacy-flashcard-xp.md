# ADR-016: Evaluate legacy flashcard XP before a real learner rollout

- Date: 2026-10-24 (extraction; source records earlier decisions)
- Status: accepted (preservation of historical rationale under APPROVAL-01)
- Decided by: Fred, approval to preserve the accepted audit's recap-only knowledge
- Source: [APPROVAL-01](../work/handover-2026/approval-01.md), [original recap](../archive/session-recaps/MEMORY.md), [inventory section 2](../work/handover-2026/evidence/audit-docs-inventory.md#2-knowledge-surviving-only-in-session-recaps-or-local-history)

## Context
The Phase 5 switch derives flashcard XP from counted reviews; earlier flashcard evidence no longer adds XP. Historical test-account balances could therefore drop.

## Decision
Preserve the handover gate: if real learners have pre-Phase-5 flashcard history, a named human must decide whether to grandfather it. The grandfathering outcome remains undecided; no historical data rewrite is authorized.

## Consequences
Historical test-only observations cannot establish safe treatment of future real learner histories. WI-08 must capture this rollout condition.

## Alternatives considered
Do not automatically backfill old evidence or assume every future account is a test account.
