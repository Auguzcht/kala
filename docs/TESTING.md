# Manual smoke test checklist

About 55 minutes. Use a test course with one student account and one instructor account. Open the
course from the learning system so each account starts in the right course.
The Review due and Study all steps expect those two actions to be available on the updated Study
screen. If they are not visible yet, mark those steps as waiting for the student-screen update.
XP shown on test accounts may drop after Phase 5 because old flashcard reviews no longer count
toward XP. That is expected: flashcard XP now comes from counted review records, while the old
flashcard events remain available for research.

## Student — about 37 minutes

| Done | Time | What to click | What should happen |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ☐ | 2 min | Open the course link in the learning system and launch Kala as a student. | Kala opens in the right course. The course name is correct and the main page loads without an error. |
| ☐ | 2 min | Click **Skills**, then open a skill. | The skill page opens and shows its lesson, study cards, and test. |
| ☐ | 3 min | Open the skill’s **Study** list. Note the total and the counts for New, Learning, Slipping, Known, and Due today. Mark one card **Got it**, return to the list, and refresh it. | The total card count stays the same. The card remains in the list, and its state or due count may change. |
| ☐ | 4 min | Choose **Review due** from Study. Turn over a card, then choose **Review again** on one card. | The answer appears after turning the card. The missed card comes back before the session ends. Other cards continue in the session. |
| ☐ | 4 min | Start another session and choose **Study all**. Turn over a card that is not due and choose **Got it**. If there are more than 20 cards, continue with the next group. | Study all includes cards beyond the due list, gives no more than 20 cards in one session, continues beyond the daily new-card allowance, and does not hide cards from the Study list. |
| ☐ | 2 min | In a fresh session, choose **Review again** on a card, then choose **Got it** when that card returns. | The card returns after “Review again.” “Got it” records the rating and moves the session forward. |
| ☐ | 5 min | Open **Test** for a skill. Start one set of 5 questions, then 10, then 20. Answer each set and return to the skill between sets. | Each set has the number chosen. Answers receive feedback, and the set finishes without missing or repeating questions unexpectedly. |
| ☐ | 5 min | Open **Diagnostic** from the course page. Answer the first question and continue through the diagnostic. | Questions load, answers can be selected and submitted, and the final result appears. No study card rating is requested. |
| ☐ | 4 min | Open a **Lesson** from a skill. Read to a lesson check and submit an answer. | The lesson loads, the check accepts an answer, and feedback appears. You can continue the lesson afterward. |
| ☐ | 3 min | Open **Tutor** from the course page. Ask a short course question and send it. | Kala returns an answer in the conversation. The page stays usable while the answer loads. |

## Instructor — about 18 minutes

| Done | Time | What to click | What should happen |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ☐ | 2 min | Launch the same course as an instructor and open **Class**. Look at the student list. | The roster loads with enrolled students. Student names and course status are visible. |
| ☐ | 2 min | Click a student’s name to open the student sheet. | The sheet shows that student’s course standing and next steps without leaving the class page. |
| ☐ | 3 min | In the sheet, open the decision area. Record a test decision and a short note, close the sheet, then open it again. | The saved decision and note are still there. The student remains in the roster. |
| ☐ | 4 min | Return to **Class** and open **Skill mapping and review**. Review one waiting skill. | The review list shows the proposed skill and where it came from. Approving or rejecting a test proposal updates the list and the waiting count. |
| ☐ | 3 min | Click **Refresh skills** and wait for the status message. | Kala reports that the refresh has started, then shows progress or says the list is up to date. The page remains usable while it runs. |
| ☐ | 4 min | Open the **Heatmap** tab. Find a student and a skill, then refresh the page. | The heatmap loads with the same skill and student rows after refresh. Cells show the course’s current skill results. |

## Finish

- ☐ Reopen the student Study list. Confirm the card total still matches the list and includes Known cards.
- ☐ Confirm the instructor can still open the roster, student sheet, skill review list, and heatmap.
- ☐ Write down any step that failed, the course, the account role, and what appeared on screen.
